Project 09 — Baglyra, long-form documentary

Will Humanoid Robots Take Your Job by 2030?

My first long-form documentary for Baglyra, just under six minutes. Humanoid robots are no longer a demo, companies are renting them by the month. Agility's Digit costs about $8,500 a month, roughly $16 an hour next to a $30 warehouse wage, and Figure 02 is already working the line at BMW. I traced the deals, the deployments and the economics, then built the film around one question: when the robot costs less than the human, what happens to the job?

How it was made: the whole film runs through one connected pipeline, Claude to Higgsfield to the final cut. Claude and Higgsfield talk through Higgsfield's MCP connector. I add it once in Claude, sign in to my Higgsfield account, and from then on everything happens in one conversation. No API key, no switching apps. I direct every step. The tools do the heavy lifting, I keep the craft.

  1. Research. I pull the facts first, from primary sources: company filings, earnings, verified reporting. Every number on screen is checked before it goes in the script.
  2. Script with Claude. I write the story with Claude as my writing partner: hook first, thesis, one jaw-dropping stat early, lesson at the end. The narration comes first and is timed to the second, and every visual is built to fit the voice.
  3. Visuals in Higgsfield. I lock the look with a reference image first, so the robot, the warehouse and the light stay consistent from shot to shot. Then I direct every shot in Higgsfield with Seedance 2.5, generating at low resolution to save credits, then upscaling to 1080. Claude splits the brief into shots and picks the model when I let it. I check the credit cost before each batch. I prompt, judge each frame, and regenerate anything that looks off. Nothing ships on the first generation.
  4. Cut and finish. I cut the film to the narration, add kinetic type and motion, then check a timestamped contact sheet frame by frame. Anything off goes back through Claude for a Higgsfield fix, an upscale, a reframe, or a fresh generation. Finished generations land in my Higgsfield Assets, ready to download. Finished in 4K, published in Full HD 1080p.

Result: published November 23, 2026 on YouTube in Full HD 1080p, with a Facebook version the same day. My first long-form, and the start of a weekly documentary series.

Tools: Higgsfield · Seedance 2.5 · Claude · MCP
