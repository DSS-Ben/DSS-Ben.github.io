Project 09 — Baglyra Black Friday, AI video & motion

That SALE price might be lying to you.

An 80-second Baglyra short I made for Black Friday. Almost 1 in 3 Amazon Black Friday deals were no cheaper than the month before, so the video shows how to spot a fake discount: check the price history first. I verified every number on screen against the Groundwork Collaborative report, the Which? analysis and the Adobe Analytics forecast, then built the story around the one stat that matters.

How it was made: I researched and scripted the story with Claude, generated the visuals in Higgsfield, and cut everything in Premiere Pro, voiceover, kinetic type and pacing included. Claude and Higgsfield are my daily tools. I prompt, direct and judge every frame, the AI does the heavy lifting on generation, I keep the craft.

Result: published November 23, 2026, the week of Black Friday, to YouTube Shorts, Instagram, Facebook and Threads, in Full HD.

Tools: Higgsfield · Claude · Premiere Pro
