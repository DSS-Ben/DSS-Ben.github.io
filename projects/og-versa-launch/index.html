Project 01 — E-commerce launch · Landing page

New products, introduced the way people actually arrive.

This launch was built to introduce new products on an e-commerce website. It starts in a newsletter — someone clicks, and lands here. The page carries them in order: the pods first, then the battery that powers them. Flavour first, hardware second, so the choice feels easy before the tech gets explained.

That order is the thinking behind the whole page. A newsletter click is warm intent, not cold browsing, so the landing page should not make people hunt. Show what is new, let them pick a flavour, then reveal the device that runs it — and only then ask them to shop.

How it works

One click, one journey, in the order a customer actually thinks:

01

Newsletter

The email announces the new products and links straight here — no homepage detour.

02

Pods first

The OG Series lineup, five flavours, in a drag-and-scroll carousel you can actually use.

03

Battery next

The Versa X device, revealed by scrolling — it grows, and its power, battery and e-liquid gauges fill as you go.

04

Shop

Only after the product story is clear does the page point to the collection.

Try it live, below.

Scroll through the pods, then keep scrolling into the battery section. The device is pinned and scales with your scroll — drag the carousel, use the arrows or dots, and watch the gauges fill. It is fully responsive: on a phone the cards stack and the carousel becomes a swipe.
