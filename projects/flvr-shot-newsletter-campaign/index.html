FLVR SHOT newsletter campaign

Project 08 · Brand · Email design · Photoshop · Illustrator

A bilingual newsletter campaign for FLVR SHOT, the 10 ml flavour shot. Sixteen flavours, each with its own colour world: the bottle, the fruit, the splash, and one big bilingual headline, built as a repeatable system so every new flavour slots straight in. Designed in Photoshop and Illustrator, English and French throughout.

How the system works

One layout, sixteen worlds

Same grid, same headline stack, same Order Now button. Only the colour, the fruit and the splash change, so the campaign reads as one brand, not sixteen one-offs.

Bilingual, EN / FR

Every headline and button is English and French, set in the same condensed type, so the French never feels like an afterthought.

Built in Photoshop & Illustrator

Product cutouts, splashes and type laid out in Photoshop, label and logo work in Illustrator. Exported for email at 1000 px wide.
