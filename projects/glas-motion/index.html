Project 07 — Motion graphics, animation

Glās in motion.

No generation, no shortcuts. Every frame here was built by hand — drawn in Illustrator and Photoshop, animated in After Effects, on classic animation fundamentals: timing, anticipation, squash and stretch, and follow-through. Four vertical spots and a logo sting for Glās — Ramen, Snacks & Soda — each one moved the old-school way.

Result: a complete motion set for one brand — the same bold colours, cartoon humour, and brand voice across every spot, plus a sting to open and close them. Hand-animated end to end, no AI anywhere in the pipeline.

Tools: After Effects · Photoshop · Illustrator
