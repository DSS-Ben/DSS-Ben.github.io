Project 10 — Vape at Home, motion graphics explainer

The numbers, in motion.

VapeAtHome.ca runs on Shopify, and selling vape there keeps getting harder. Instead of a boring PDF, we showed the client the numbers in motion graphics: the plan fees, the gateway cut at every sales level, and what the move to WooCommerce saves. Built in After Effects.

Result: the recommendation is to move to WooCommerce. At $50,000 a month in sales, up to $12,000 saved a year, with no platform fee and no cut of sales.

Tools: After Effects
