Project 02 — Nasty Bundle 2 in 1, wholesale promotion

The bundle that makes more sense.

A promotion for wholesale buyers, with one point to land: buying the bundle is cheaper than buying a 20ml disposable. One device body, many pods — a lower entry price, and margin that repeats with every refill. The comparison below does the selling, side by side.

Result: the project was successful and resulted in a spike in sales. The client was very satisfied.
