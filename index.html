Process

  1. 01

    Listen

    I start by listening — your audience, your shelf, your competitors — before I open a file.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    I sketch on paper first — many directions, narrowed down to the two worth building.

  3. 03

    Refine

    I lock grid, type and colour, and I check every margin at real size, on screen and in print.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    I deliver press-ready files, a usage guide, and a press check when it goes to print.
