Process

  1. 01

    Listen

    I start by listening — your audience, your shelf, your competitors — before I open a file.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    I sketch on paper first — many directions, narrowed down to the two worth building.

  3. 03

    Refine

    I lock grid, type and colour, and I check every margin at real size, on screen and in print.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    I deliver press-ready files, a usage guide, and a press check when it goes to print.

I'm a graphic designer and, at my core, an artist. Nearly ten years in, I still sketch first, obsess over the details, and want to get better with every project. I'm looking for a good company where I can keep developing my skills — and bring everything I've learned to the table.

My take on AI

I know AI is going to play an essential role in the future of design, and I use it every day. But I still believe mastering the fundamentals is just as important.

I learned to draw, to look closely, and to think through a problem before I ever touched an AI tool, and that is what keeps my work mine.

For me, AI is a tool to spark more creativity, support how I work through a project, and turn an idea into something real. It is not a substitute for design skills, critical thinking, or human creativity. The goal is to use AI to make better work, not to let it do all the work for me.
