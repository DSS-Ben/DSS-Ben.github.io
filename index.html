Process

  1. 01

    Listen

    Your audience, your shelf and your competitors — before a single file is opened.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    Pencil first. Dozens of rough directions, narrowed together to the two worth building.

  3. 03

    Refine

    Grid, type and colour get locked. Every margin checked at real size, on screen and in print.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Press-ready files, a usage guide, and a press check if it's going to print.

About

Ben Alvis is a graphic designer based in Montreal, working in brand identity, packaging, editorial, motion and e-commerce. He builds brand systems, packaging and digital campaigns end to end — combining strong fundamentals in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign with an AI-native production pipeline and hands-on 3D prototyping.
