Process

  1. 01

    Listen

    Audience, shelf and competitors first — before a file is opened.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    Many directions on paper, narrowed to the two worth building.

  3. 03

    Refine

    Grid, type and colour locked. Margins checked at real size, on screen and in print.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Press-ready files, a usage guide, and a press check when it goes to print.
